    </div>
    <div style="display: flex; gap: 32px; flex-grow: 1; align-items: center;">
      <div style="flex: 1; display: flex; flex-direction: column; gap: 20px;">
        <div style="background: #f8fafc; border: 1px solid #e2e8f0; border-radius: 12px; padding: 22px;">
          <h3 contenteditable="true" style="font-size: 18px; font-weight: 600; color: #0f172a; margin-bottom: 6px;">Safety Boundary</h3>
          <p contenteditable="true" style="font-size: 16px; color: #475569; margin: 0;">Passenger acceleration strictly restricted between -0.5g (top) and +2.0g (bottom) for children's safety.</p>
        </div>
        <div style="background: #f8fafc; border: 1px solid #e2e8f0; border-radius: 12px; padding: 22px;">
          <h3 contenteditable="true" style="font-size: 18px; font-weight: 600; color: #0f172a; margin-bottom: 6px;">Radius Constraint</h3>
          <p contenteditable="true" style="font-size: 16px; color: #475569; margin: 0;">With a wheel radius $R = 7\text{m}$, bottom limit yields $\omega \le 1.186\text{ rad/s}$.</p>
        </div>
        <div style="background: #f8fafc; border: 1px solid #e2e8f0; border-radius: 12px; padding: 22px;">
          <h3 contenteditable="true" style="font-size: 18px; font-weight: 600; color: #0f172a; margin-bottom: 6px;">Optimal RPM</h3>
          <p contenteditable="true" style="font-size: 16px; color: #475569; margin: 0;">Operating speed set to approximately 11.3 RPM (or controlled slow rotation) to guarantee comfort.</p>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div style="flex: 1; background: #0f172a; border-radius: 16px; padding: 36px; color: #ffffff; display: flex; flex-direction: column; justify-content: center; gap: 24px;">
        <h3 contenteditable="true" style="font-size: 22px; font-weight: 700; color: #38bdf8;">Kinematic Equations</h3>
        <p contenteditable="true" style="font-size: 18px; line-height: 1.6; color: #cbd5e1; margin: 0;">
          Top limit: $g_{top} = g - \omega^2 R \ge -0.5g$<br>
          Bottom limit: $g_{bot} = g + \omega^2 R \le 2.0g$<br>
          Resulting Max Velocity: $\omega = 1.186\text{ rad/s}$ ($11.3\text{ RPM}$)
        </p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
